About Tuck In

The tuckshop on the ground floor of Block C, next to the library.

Why we take orders online

At lunch the queue for russian rolls used to reach the stairs, and most people only have a short break between lectures. Ordering ahead means the kitchen can start your food before you arrive, so you collect it and go.

The counter still works the old way too: walk up, order, pay. Online orders are simply packed first at the time you choose.

How the shop has grown

  1. 2019

    Opened with a grill, a fridge and eight items on the board.

  2. 2021

    Added boerewors rolls and a second fryer for slap chips.

  3. 2023

    Started taking card payments at the counter.

  4. 2026

    Started taking orders online.

Questions

Do I need an account to order?

You can look at the menu and add items without one. You need an account to send the order, so we can show you when it is ready.

How do I pay?

Cash or card at the counter when you collect. Nothing is charged online.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, from My orders, as long as the kitchen has not started it. After that, speak to us at the counter.

What happens if I don't collect?

We keep orders until closing time. Orders that are not collected too often may be refused.
